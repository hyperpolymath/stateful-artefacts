The maintainers of stateful-artefacts commit that this software is developed
and published with the following expectations of use:

1. The software must not be used to cause, coordinate, or conceal harm to
   people — including surveillance, discrimination, or exploitation.
2. Provenance and state metadata produced by this software must not be
   falsified to misrepresent the origin, verification status, or lifecycle
   of an artefact.
3. Downstream distributions should preserve this exhibit alongside the
   MPL-2.0 licence text (licensing/texts/MPL-2.0.txt).

This exhibit expresses the maintainers' intent. It does not modify the
