The maintainers of stateful-artefacts commit to the following with respect to
cryptographic agility:

1. Any signing or hashing of artefact state records introduced by this
   project will prefer post-quantum-ready primitives where mature
   implementations exist, and will never hard-code a single algorithm
   without an upgrade path.
2. Record formats reserve space for algorithm identifiers so that
   signatures and digests can migrate without breaking readers.
3. Deprecated primitives will be called out in CHANGELOG.md and SECURITY.md
   with a migration window.
